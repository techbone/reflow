# Reflow sharing terms: Arc Mainnet activity report v1.0.0

These terms are committed on-chain as the pool's `termsHash` (keccak256 of this file's exact bytes). Changing a single character produces a different hash, so these terms cannot be changed after the pool is created. A new version is a new pool.

## 1. What this is

- **Resource:** `arc-activity-report-mainnet-v1.json`, a summary of public **Arc Mainnet** activity for a fixed block range stated in the file and in the manifest, generated by the open generator script published with the project.
- **Commitment:** the pool's `artifactHash` is the keccak256 of that file's exact bytes. After delivery you can compute the hash yourself and compare it to the pool on-chain.
- **Provider:** the wallet that created the pool (the pool's `provider` on-chain). Contact: open an issue at https://github.com/techbone/reflow.

## 2. Real USDC

This pool runs on **Arc Mainnet** and is priced in **real USDC**. Every payment is a real transfer of value. The data is public chain data that anyone can regenerate with the generator script, so you are paying for a packaged, hash-committed copy and for the cost-sharing mechanism, not for exclusive information. Only pay an amount you are comfortable losing.

## 3. Cohort licence

Each wallet recorded as a member of the pool by the contract may use the delivered file for personal or internal review. You may quote short excerpts with attribution. You may **not** publish, resell, or redistribute the file outside the pool's members.

Membership lets the delivery service release the file to your wallet. It cannot stop you copying the file elsewhere; the licence above is the only restriction, and it relies on you honouring it.

## 4. Cost sharing

- The first buyer pays the provider the full price of 1.20 USDC. The provider is paid once.
- Each later buyer pays an equal-share contribution into the contract, which credits earlier buyers so every member's net cost converges to equal shares (within one micro-USDC of rounding).
- Rebates are paid only if later buyers join before the pool deadline or the cap is reached. Nothing is promised beyond what the contract enforces. A rebate you have earned stays claimable by you after the deadline.

## 5. Risks you accept

- **Unaudited code.** The contract has automated tests and a developer review but has not been audited by an outside firm.
- **No refunds.** Version 1 has no refund or dispute process other than the contract's own rebate rules. If the provider does not deliver, the contract does not return your payment.
- **USDC controls.** USDC can be paused or an address blocklisted by its issuer. If that happens, a payment or rebate claim involving that address, or the pool contract, can fail or stall until the issuer acts. The contract has no administrator and cannot override this.
- **Stranded transfers.** Tokens sent directly to the contract outside the purchase functions cannot be recovered.

## 6. Delivery and warranty

Delivery is through a service that checks a wallet signature and your membership on-chain. It is best effort: the provider keeps a copy and will try to restore access if the service is down, but offers no uptime guarantee. The file is provided as is, without warranty of accuracy, completeness, or fitness for any purpose.
